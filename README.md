## Project overview

BridgeMind is a proposed generative-AI decision-support framework intended to connect four evidence domains:

1. **Bridge condition and asset data** — structural inventory and inspection indicators.
2. **Transportation network function** — connectivity, criticality, and potential detour implications.
3. **Hazard exposure** — such as flood or extreme-weather exposure where suitable data are available.
4. **Community vulnerability** — social vulnerability and access to alternative routes.

The proposed workflow combines structured indicators and transparent screening logic with AI-assisted evidence summaries. The goal is to help engineering and infrastructure decision-makers compare candidate rehabilitation and recovery priorities while keeping assumptions, data gaps, and trade-offs visible.

BridgeMind is intended to **support—not replace—professional engineering judgment**.

## Interactive sandbox: important limitation

The on-page scoring sandbox uses **synthetic, illustrative values** to explain how a configurable screening workflow might behave. It is not connected to live bridge records, has not been presented as a validated risk model, and must not be used for operational safety, inspection, funding, or rehabilitation decisions.

A research implementation would require, at minimum:

- Documented data provenance and quality controls
- Transparent indicator definitions and calibration
- Sensitivity and robustness analysis
- Validation against engineering judgment and relevant agency practices
- Assessment of uncertainty, bias, and equity implications
- Human review of all generated summaries and recommendations
